01About

Data systems that work on the ground.

I set up and strengthen health data systems, make sure the data in them can be trusted, and turn it into reports, dashboards and maps that management, government and partners can act on.

My work spans field assessments, emergency response, tool validation and clinical trials. I work with government at federal, provincial and local levels and with vulnerable and marginalized communities, always in line with ethical, safeguarding and data protection standards.
